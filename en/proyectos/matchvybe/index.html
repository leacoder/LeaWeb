The idea

Vybe connects people through something they already share: an event, a community or a place. The app lets people discover who is attending and start a conversation before meeting.

My contribution

I defined the concept and turned it into a web application. I handled the user experience and interface design, frontend and backend development, database and chat.

The work covered both the user-facing experience and the logic connecting accounts, profiles, plans and conversations within the product.

What’s included

  • Accounts and profiles to discover other people.
  • Discovery of events, communities and places.
  • Matching through mutual interest and chat before meeting.
  • Database and backend supporting the application’s features.
  • Tools for organizers to manage and showcase their events.

Web design and development for small businesses

Web design and development for small businesses and entrepreneurs in Argentina.
Responsive websites with technical SEO, from concept and design to launch.

Tell me about your project ↗

A website that explains what you do

Your website should help people understand your offer, discover your work and find a way to contact you. I work with small businesses and entrepreneurs in Argentina to turn that need into a clearly structured website with its own identity.

We can start with an idea, an existing design or a website that needs updating. First, we define what it needs to solve; then we choose the pages and features it needs.

When is this service a good fit?

If you need to introduce your business, showcase services or receive inquiries online, we can plan a landing page or a multi-page website. Responsive design adapts content to phones, tablets and computers; headings, links and metadata help search engines understand each page.

Before building, we agree on the visitor’s intended action, the content you have and who will update the site. Hosting, domain and features follow that scope. Technical SEO is part of the foundation; visibility is assessed after launch and also depends on content and competition.

If you need to manage processes or connect tools, explore software and integrations. If you already have a website and want to update it, we can improve your existing project. In the portfolio, you can explore Librería 2001, a retail website.

Project stages

  1. Understand the business. We review your goals, your audience and the content you have available.
  2. Define the project. We agree on scope, priorities and deliverables before development begins.
  3. Design and build. We organize content, develop the visual identity and review the site across screen sizes.
  4. Prepare for launch. We check navigation, contact options and technical details so search engines can access the content.

Frequently asked questions

Can I start before all my copy is ready?

Yes. In our first conversation, we identify what material is available and what’s missing. Copywriting, photography and other content preparation can be included in the scope when needed.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. The design adapts to different devices and is reviewed on small screens from the start.

Does it include Google search optimization?

Development includes a technical SEO foundation: accessible content, headings, structure and carefully prepared images. Search rankings also depend on content, competition and ongoing work; no specific position is promised.

How is the price determined?

The price depends on the pages, features and materials required. With a description of your business and needs, we can agree on a concrete scope and proposal.

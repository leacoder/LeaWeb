05 / BEFORE WE START

Your first
questions.

Every project is different.
These answers help you take the first step.

What is LG Design and who does the work?+

LG Design is the brand of Leandro García, a digital consultant, designer and developer.
I work remotely with small businesses and entrepreneurs in Argentina to create websites, develop software and integrations, or improve and complete existing projects.

Can you join a project that has already started?+

Yes. First, I review the project’s current state and what you need to resolve.
Based on that assessment, we decide what to keep, improve or complete, and agree on the scope before moving forward.

Do you work with clients across Argentina?+

Yes, I work remotely.
We can arrange meetings, review progress and share project materials wherever your business is based.

How is the price determined?+

It depends on the scope and the starting point.
Once I understand your needs, I prepare a proposal with deliverables, milestones and terms.
For larger projects, we can work in phases.

What do you need to get started?+

A conversation about what you want to achieve.
If you already have a website, software, designs or documentation, that material helps.
If you only have an idea, we can start by giving it shape.
